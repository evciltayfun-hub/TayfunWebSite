The engineer who treats water like a living system.

At the intersection of rigorous chemistry and industrial reality, Tayfun Evcil has built a career that refuses to separate theory from consequence. Water treatment isn't abstract when it's keeping a geothermal power plant online or protecting a steel mill's cooling circuit from scale.

Currently at Solenis as an Industrial Solutions Applications Specialist, he designs chemical treatment programs for some of the most demanding environments in the MEA and CIS regions — from SWRO desalination systems to high-temperature boiler circuits and large-scale cooling loops. Every program is backed by verified field data, never approximated.

Beyond the field, Tayfun builds his own tools — Python-based simulation platforms that run water chemistry models in-house, cutting the dependency on expensive third-party software. The most complete of them is AQUIONIS, a cooling tower intelligence platform with a deterministic calculation core and an AI assistant that is never allowed to invent a number. And increasingly, he uses AI not as a shortcut but as a force multiplier: faster documentation, better proposals, sharper technical reports.

01 / Flagship Platform

AQUIONIS

Cooling Tower Intelligence Platform — Python / Flask / Claude · Private

A cooling water treatment platform built around one rule: every number comes from a deterministic calculation core, never from guesswork. It takes a makeup water analysis through a guided wizard, validates it, finds how far the water can be cycled, and turns the chemistry into a clear risk picture and a treatment projection.

Cooling Water Deterministic Engine Data Validation Claude Tool Use Private Platform
  • Gate-1 validationEvery analysis is checked before anything is calculated: ion balance, impossible or out-of-range values, and units. Hardness and alkalinity are handled on a CaCO₃ basis, with lab units such as °dH, °f and gpg converted by the app's own tables.
  • Cycles & scaling engineFinds the cycles-of-concentration limit and the species that sets it, then calculates LSI at bulk and heat-transfer skin temperature, Ryznar, silica saturation and Larson-Skold. The worst index drives a single Low / Medium / High risk rating, and water that cannot be cycled at all is flagged as such.
  • AI assistant that never invents numbersA built-in Claude assistant answers in plain language but calculates nothing itself. It calls the engine's own tools for unit conversion, ion-balance closure and what-if scenarios, and every call is shown in an Engine Calls panel so each figure can be checked.
  • Engineer stays in controlThe assistant works on a copy of the project and can only propose changes; nothing is applied until the engineer approves it. Balance-closing values are labelled "assumed" and never written into the project, and scenarios that fail validation are stopped. All calculations and reports work without the AI layer.

06 / Engineering Software

RO Optimizer

Web App — Python / Flask / PHREEQC

Reverse osmosis scaling and recovery tool. Splits a raw water analysis into feed, permeate and brine streams at a chosen recovery, then calculates mineral saturation with the USGS PHREEQC engine, treating CO₂ as a closed system across the membrane. Reports LSI, Ryznar, Stiff-Davis, Puckorius and Larson-Skold indices and suggests an antiscalant.

Reverse Osmosis PHREEQC Scaling Indices

GeoWell Assessment

Geothermal Field Tool — Python / Flask

Chemical cleaning assessment for geothermal wells, built for field work in Kenya. Calculates flash point and steam quality, gives reservoir temperature from silica, Na/K and K/Mg geothermometers, checks brine charge balance and mineral saturation along the well depth, and sizes wellbore volumes and the treatment split.
